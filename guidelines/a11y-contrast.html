AA floor: 4.5:1 text · 3:1 large text and control boundaries

Ratios below are measured, not estimated. Black is never placed on Crisp Orange — not text, not icons. Filled orange carries Finisher White at 2.6:1, so an orange fill is only used for large or semibold labels and never for small body copy; where AA text on an orange field is required, use the pressed step --lofty-orange-pressed. Control boundaries use --ui-border-color (3.5:1 on white); Flint 300 is the card, panel and table rule, which carries no information and is exempt. Mid Grey draws nothing in the UI.
