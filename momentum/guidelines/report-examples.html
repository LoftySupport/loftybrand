Reports › Budget, in the staff app

The series colours in use on a real app screen. Sample figures only.

  • Spend by trade: stacked bars, one series colour per trade in fixed order, 2px gaps, only the data end rounded, totals in ink beside the bar.
  • Where the money went: a donut in the same trade order, with a neutral slice for anything outside the five series.
  • Cost to date: one line per project in series order, with direct labels at the line end and a legend.
  • Variations: grouped bars, Approved (series-1) and Awaiting client (series-2).
  • Status colours stay on status chips; the AI tile keeps plum. No chart uses Eco Green, plum, blush or sky.
