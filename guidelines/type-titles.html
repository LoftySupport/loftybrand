How the steps hold up
Size alone is a weak signal — 32 to 24 to 18 is only one step of contrast each. Three things carry the hierarchy: bold titles against normal-weight body, Foundation Black against secondary grey, and space above a heading roughly double the space below it, so each title binds to the text it introduces. Drop any one and the ladder flattens.
