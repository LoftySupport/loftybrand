# AnswerWidget

How the AI answers: one sentence, then the record as widgets, then the action.

- Up to three tiles in a row: a progress ring tile, a figure tile, and one accent tile (plum) for the headline risk.
- Figures in Outfit 600 with negative tracking; labels in `caption` muted.
- Follow with a blocker row on `surface-input` holding the single orange action.
- Keep the sentence under 20 words. Numbers must come from the record, never estimated.
