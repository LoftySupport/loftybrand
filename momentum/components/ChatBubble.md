# ChatBubble

Who said what, at a glance. Keep it quiet: the answer widgets carry the colour, not the bubbles.

- **You:** right-aligned, `surface-input` fill, 1px `line-control` edge, `ink` text at weight 500, `radius-l` with a 4px tail corner bottom right. White on light, a light wash on dark.
- **Lofty:** no bubble. A 28 to 32px plum tile with the orange Lofty arrows (`plum-lift` on dark), then plain `body` text and any widgets.
- Keep your own messages short; the AI's text sits on the frame, not in a box.
- Not orange: the send button is the page's one orange action. Not plum: that is the AI's colour.

Decided 24 September 2026, from Grey, Eco Green, Current, Plum and Orange options.
