## Fieldwork

`assets/fonts/` contains six Fieldwork `.woff` files, used for brand-led
surfaces (decks, print, proposals, hero statements). They ship with this
repository by decision of the brand owner.

Fieldwork is a **commercial typeface**. Committing webfont binaries is a
distribution, and foundry licences commonly bound distribution by domain,
pageview or seat count. This repository is private; keep it that way, or
confirm the Lofty Fieldwork licence covers wider distribution before opening
it up.
