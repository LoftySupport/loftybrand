Lofty Hub · app icon · chosen 26 September 2026

Chosen: Gantt, tone on tone, all orange

Three programme bars in Crisp Orange's own shades: done and in progress in Orange Pressed, the next task in white, each ending in the Lofty point. It stays all orange and the white bar leads the eye forward.

Round 5 follows your feedback: orange-only Gantt versions and the L from the Lofty wordmark. L programme, which puts the Gantt bars inside the L, is the strongest of the L ideas. The Pinwheel stays as the teal alternative.

Round 3

Ten geometric concepts, graded

Grades judge recognition at 20pt, how Lofty it feels, whether it points forward, and whether it survives the tinted iOS mode. The weakest are kept in so you can see what was ruled out.

Gantt, teal

A-

Three programme bars in teal shades, each ending in the Lofty point. Reads as a Gantt chart at once and moves forward. The stagger is even, so it is a little mechanical.

Gantt, orange

B+

Same bars on orange in white, Blush and Deep Eco. Strong contrast from the Deep Eco bar, but three unrelated colours make it busier than the teal.

Pinwheel, orange shades

B+

The pinwheel you liked, now in four shades from white to Deep Eco, so the eye travels round it. Still reads a little like a loading spinner.

Pinwheel, teal shades

A-

Four teal shades on Eco Green. Calmer and more like a hub drawing work in. The darkest blade gets close to the ground, which weakens it at small sizes.

Petal hub

B

Quarter circles and Lofty triangles in a 2 x 2 grid, like the yellow reference. Friendly and geometric, but the curves pull away from the sharp Lofty character.

Forward diamond

A-

Your first reference, rebuilt with 45-degree Lofty angles: a solid half pushing into a chevron. Bold, reads as "go", and survives a single colour. It is close to the reference, so it needs its own twist.

Hub H

B-

An H with a forward bar through it. Clear letterform and one orange action. The orange bar overlapping white is fussy, and H alone does not say Lofty.

Hub node

B

Hub and spokes: a centre with four Lofty corners pointing out. Says "hub" literally. Symmetric, so it loses the forward direction the brand relies on.

Four-way seam

B

The seam square split four ways, with the right-hand triangle pushed forward in orange. Neat, but at 20pt it reads as an envelope.

L block

C

An L for Lofty from a bar, a quarter circle and a triangle. The weakest here: the three pieces do not lock together, and it reads as an L, not an app.

Round 4

Five revisions

  • Dropped Petal hub, Hub H, Hub node, Four-way seam and L block: they soften the Lofty angles, spell a letter, or read as an envelope.
  • Gantt: uneven task lengths like a real programme, with the next task in orange.
  • Pinwheel: the lead blade in orange gives it a start; an all-white orange version shows the one-colour option.
  • Forward diamond: moved to teal with an orange chevron so it is no longer a copy of the reference.

Gantt, revised

A-

Uneven lengths, like a real programme: a finished task, one in progress, and the next task in orange, pointing forward. Clearest story of the set. The pointed ends can still read as tags, so a flat-ended version is worth testing.

Gantt, orange revised

A-

The same programme on orange, with the next task in Deep Eco. Warmer and more on brand, with slightly less punch than the teal.

Pinwheel, revised

A

The lead blade is now orange, so the wheel has a start and a direction, and the other three step down in teal shades. Reads as a hub that turns work over, holds up at 20pt, and keeps the Lofty triangle exact.

Pinwheel, orange revised

A-

White blades fading round the wheel on orange: one colour in four strengths, as you asked. Clean and on brand; it moves round rather than forward.

Forward diamond, revised

A-

Moved to teal with the chevron in orange: white is the work, orange is the next move. Distinct from the reference now, and it echoes the send button in the app.

Before you choose

Orange versions

White on Crisp Orange measures 2.6:1, below the 3:1 guide for graphics. The teal versions sit on Eco Green, where white is 9.2:1. The lighter glow in the top corner brings the pinwheel's white blade down to 3.1:1 at its tip, which still clears the 3:1 guide.

A new brand mark

The design system says to use the arrows as supplied. The chosen icon uses the Lofty point in a new arrangement, so it needs sign-off as a new mark before it ships.

Finish in Icon Composer

The icon kit now has Gantt, tone on tone as separate layers for iOS 26, plus flattened 1024px PNGs with dark and tinted versions and the bar measurements.
