Menu and account

The header's two panels. The menu button opens Home, Files and Contact with the current page selected; the account button opens the signed-in person, the address, the access date and Log out. Opening one closes the other.

Reference screens from the portal handoff package of 26 September 2026, drawn in Momentum 1.4 with Montserrat. The built app and its later changes are in portal/README.md in loftysupport/loftyprojectapp.
