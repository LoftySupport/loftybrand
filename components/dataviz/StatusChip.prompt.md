# StatusChip

The status treatment for tables, boards and dashboards.

```jsx
<StatusChip label="On site" tone="positive" />
<StatusChip label="Overdue" tone="negative" />
```

Transparent fill, Flint 400 border, Foundation Black text, 8px dot. The dot carries the colour so the text keeps its contrast on both themes. `Label` is the filled variant and belongs to calls to action, not states.
