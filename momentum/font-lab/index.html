Lofty Momentum · body typeface test

Is it the font, or the colours?

Body text in Montserrat is hard to read on the Lofty themes, most of all in Deep Eco and Twilight. Two things could be causing it: the typeface (how big, wide and heavy its letters are) and the colours (how bright the text is against the ground, with the glows and glass behind it). This tester separates them on the real Sunrise, Deep Eco and Twilight grounds, with Fieldwork, the Lofty brand face, as the yardstick.

Change one thing at a time. Text colours and Backgrounds in the top bar change the colours; the two font selectors below change the typeface. Contrast ratios depend on colour only, so they do not move when you change the font. Fieldwork is supplied in Light (300) and DemiBold (600) only: weights 400 and 500 show Light. Nothing in the system changes until you choose.

Chips under each tile give the contrast at the brightest glow behind the text (4.5:1 is the AA pass for body text).

Contrast by combination

Computed from the Momentum tokens and the grounds the Hub uses. Ink is headings and body text, muted is captions, table cells and nav. Each figure is taken at the brightest glow behind the text, behind the glass card. The same numbers apply to every font.

Measured against Fieldwork

Taken from the font files themselves (7 October 2026, font-lab/metrics.json). Lowercase height decides how large a face looks at a given size. Width decides how much room words take. Stroke weight at 400, as a share of lowercase height, decides how solid small text looks, and it is what vanishes first in dark mode. Closeness scores how near each face stays to Fieldwork on width, letter proportions and stroke weight.

Sample text is invented for the test. Faces other than Fieldwork load from Google Fonts (open licence); Fieldwork loads from this repository's assets/fonts and is for Lofty's own use. The grounds are the Hub's (app/src/lofty-ui/surfaces.css): Sunrise #fcf1ee with orange and Current glows, Deep Eco #020a0b and Twilight #070105 with the night glows, under 42% (light) or 5% to 8% (dark) white glass. “Glows 15%” caps every glow at 15% opacity, the Momentum decision of 6 October 2026. “Readable set” is a proposal, not a token: dark ink #eef4f3, secondary text 86%, body weight one step heavier on dark, light-theme muted #3e4d50.
