# ReportExamples

ReportColours applied on a full staff-app screen: Reports > Budget, in Sunrise and Deep Eco. Sample figures only.

- Spend by trade: stacked bars in series order (Concrete and slab, Frame and roof, Services, Lock-up, Finishes).
- Where the money went: a donut in the same order, plus one neutral slice (`line-control`) for anything outside the five series.
- Cost to date: one line per project, direct labels at the line end, legend above.
- Variations: grouped bars, Approved (`series-1`) and Awaiting client (`series-2`).
- Status colours appear only on status chips. Brand accents stay on surfaces, never in the charts.
- The same screen is in the Lofty Momentum presentation, after Reports.
