1. Purpose and scope

Level two headings mark the numbered sections of the document. Body copy runs at 10.5pt over 1.6 line height, one column, ranged left. Keep paragraphs to four or five lines so a section stays scannable when it is read on screen rather than printed.

This template carries every element in the house format. Delete what a given document does not need, and keep the order of the elements you keep — the header, footer, cover block and heading levels stay as they are.

2. Background

2.1 Level three heading

Level three breaks a section into its parts. Use the section number with it so cross-references stay unambiguous.

Level four heading

Level four is a run-in label for short blocks — a caveat, a definition, a single instruction. It carries no number and no body text longer than a paragraph.

2.2 Bulleted list

Bullets carry items with no order between them. Start each with a capital and use a full stop only where the item is a full sentence.

  • One item per line, kept to a single idea.
  • Second level sits beneath its parent:
    • A supporting detail.
    • Another supporting detail.
  • No more than two levels — a third level means the section needs a heading instead.

2.3 Numbered list

Numbers are for sequence, ranking or anything that will be referred to by its number.

  1. Confirm the scope with the client in writing.
  2. Assign the crew and equipment.
  3. Issue the schedule and record the version here.
Note

A callout holds something the reader must not miss: a deadline, a safety requirement, a dependency outside our control. One per page at most, three or four lines at most.

Fonts

Two versions of this template exist. Use the brand version for anything printed or issued as a PDF, where the Fieldwork files are installed or embedded. Use the Montserrat version for any file a client will open and edit in Word.

  • Fallback order is Montserrat first, then Arial. Do not substitute Helvetica, Calibri or Aptos — the line lengths and heading sizes in this template are set against Montserrat's metrics.
  • Sending a brand-font document as .docx: tick File › Options › Save › Embed fonts in the file, and embed only the characters used. Without it Word substitutes silently and the layout reflows.
  • Fieldwork has no Regular or Medium cut. Only Light (300) and Demi Bold (600) are used, so never apply Word's synthetic bold to a Light heading.
  • Anything leaving the business as a PDF should use the brand version — the fonts travel inside the PDF and nothing can substitute them.

Space above and below

  • Level 2: 26pt above, 10pt below, then 8pt to the rule. Never leave a level 2 as the last line on a page.
  • Levels 1 to 4 are headings and sit at or above the 14pt minimum. Levels 5 and 6 are text-level labels and captions, set at body sizes.
  • Level 3: 18pt above, 8pt below. Level 4: 16pt above, 6pt below.
  • Body paragraphs: 0 above, 14pt below. Lists: 8pt above, 22pt below, 6pt between items.
  • Tables and figures: 12pt above, caption 8pt below the object, 26pt below the caption.

Using this template

  • A4, 0.7 inch margins, logo top-left and the footer line bottom of every page.
  • The cover is page one of this document. In Word, set “different first page” if the header and footer should not appear on it.
  • “Page X of Y” in the footer is a placeholder. Insert it as a page-number field in Word, or let the PDF tool number the pages.
  • Type: see Appendix B. Brand version uses Fieldwork Geo and Fieldwork Hum; the Word version uses Montserrat throughout.
  • Eco Green #005058 appears once, on the cover title header. Everything else stays black, grey and orange.
  • Colour: Crisp Orange #f47e63 is the highlight — section rules, eyebrows, callouts and pull-outs. Foundation Black #414042 for all headings and body. Status colours only inside status columns.
  • Sentence case in every heading and label. No emoji, no exclamation marks.
  • Update the version and date in the footer and the revision history every time the document is reissued.
