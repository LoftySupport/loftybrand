# StarterCard

A one-tap starting point under the prompt box. Four per screen, chosen by role.

- `surface-card` with `edge-glass`, `radius-m` (12px), 16px padding.
- A 36px icon tile in one accent, a bold title and one muted line.
- Accent by job: Eco Green for schedule, plum for what the AI is watching, orange for drafting, Current for site capture.
- Tapping fills and sends the prompt.
