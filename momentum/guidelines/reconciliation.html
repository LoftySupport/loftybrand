System reconciliation

Lofty Momentum 1.4 compared with Lofty's App Design System (the lofty-design skill, bound to this project). Every conflict is flagged with no default winner: choose Momentum, keep the App system and repo as built, or discuss. Where Momentum is silent, the App system and repo fill the gap as a proposal. Nothing here is adopted until approved.

Separation
  • The App system and LoftySupport/loftybrand are the existing, production brand. Lofty Momentum is new and stays separate from both until Momentum is approved.
  • Nothing here changes either side. Decisions are recorded as proposals only; no token, rule or file is copied across until approval.
Sources compared
  • Lofty Momentum: this project, readme.md, tokens/, docs/MOMENTUM_HANDOFF.md.
  • Lofty's App Design System: the bound _ds tree and the lofty-design skill.
  • Lofty SaaS design system: Momentum is the SaaS system, so it is compared once.
  • Repo as built: LoftySupport/loftybrand (main), the brand currently configured for the app. It matches the bound App system, plus newer additions (NavFlyout, screen pattern, data scale, record title format). Those are listed in the sections R and G-19 onward.
Kept from the original, unchanged
  • Brand: Crisp Orange, Eco Green, the Lofty lockups, favicon arrows and lines and shapes silhouettes.
  • Type families: Montserrat titles, Figtree body, Fieldwork for print, decks and brand heroes.
  • Voice: Australian English, sentence case, second person, verb-plus-object buttons, concrete numbers, no emoji, no exclamation marks.
  • Icons: 297-glyph set as a mask over currentColor, by file stem. No substitutes.
  • Orange and Eco Green never meet inside one element. Orange is never body text on white. Eco Green is not a link colour.
  • Borders are 1px. No coloured left-border strips. Press scales. Focus is never removed. Nothing bounces. Reduced motion respected.
  • WCAG 2.2 AA, 40px targets on desktop and 44px on phones.
