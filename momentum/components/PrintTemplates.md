# PrintTemplates

The Momentum cover page and document template, A4, for reports, proposals and process documents.

- **Type:** Fieldwork Geo throughout (300 body at 10.5pt on 17pt, 600 headings), tracking -0.02em.
- **Cover:** Shell page with a large Crisp Orange glow top left and a soft Current glow top right. The orange lockup top left, the Lofty arrows large at the right, the document type in Eco Green caps, the title, a one-line subtitle, and a details table (Prepared for, Prepared by, Version, Date issued, Reference, Status).
- **Header:** orange lockup at 14pt and the document title on the right, in `ink-muted`.
- **Headings:** H1 numbered with a 1px Crisp Orange rule under it; H2 numbered, no rule; H3 as a run-in label.
- **Lists:** orange triangle bullets, Current for the second level; numbered lists in orange-pressed numerals.
- **Tables:** Shell header row that repeats across page breaks, figures right-aligned, a total row in bold, status as StatusIcon chips, and a numbered caption under each table.
- **Callout:** Shell fill, 4px Crisp Orange left edge, "Note" label. One per page at most.
- **Footer:** date, version and "Commercial in confidence" on the left; "Page n of N" with the arrows on the right.
- Contents, revision history and a heading and type reference are included as appendix pages.
- Colours and fonts come from Momentum tokens; no em dashes in the template copy.
