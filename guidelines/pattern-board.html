Rebuilt below: white cards in Flint 100 lanes on a white board, so every column is a visible drop target, one 18/24 job number per card, and Crisp Orange used for the active tab and the lane you are dragging into.

What changed

Colour. Teal caps out; the board is white and each stage is a Flint 100 lane with a Flint 200 edge, so the drop zones are visible before you start dragging. The lane you are over turns orange-dashed on Flint tint. Hierarchy. One heading per card — job number 18/24, address secondary. Stage dropped; it names the column. Density. Type and status are outline buttons, not filled pills — a fill is reserved for a call to action. Owner sits in a footer, so an unowned job reads as an absence. Two different jobs, two different places. Collapse sits before the stage name as a horizontal-collapse icon — it acts on the lane. Opening the stage sits after the count as Open, so drilling a layer down is never confused with folding the column away. Collapsed, a stage becomes a 56px lane with its name vertical and its count kept, and it still accepts a dropped card.
