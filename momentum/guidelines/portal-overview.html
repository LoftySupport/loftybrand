Purchaser portal

A private, photo-led site where a purchaser logs in after handover, downloads their handover documents and contacts their Lofty team. It is a separate app on the Hub's database, with no AI. Home and Log in are photo-led; Files and Contact use the glass frame. A shared header (address, menu, account) and a footer sit on every page. Sunrise, Deep Eco and Twilight.
