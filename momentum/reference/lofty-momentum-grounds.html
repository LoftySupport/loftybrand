How to read the contrast figures
  • Each figure is the lowest contrast anywhere on the ground, measured on a 960 × 540 render, so text in the best spot reads better than the number shown.
  • Light set: Ink is Deep Eco #081A1C, 13:1 or better on every ground. Muted is #5A6668, 4.5:1 or better.
  • Dark set: Ink is #EEF4F3, 9.4:1 or better on every ground. Muted is the same colour at 68%, 5.3:1 or better. Crisp Orange text reaches 4:1 or better, so keep it for labels 14px bold and up.
  • Dark grounds sit on the two dark page colours: Deep Eco night #020A0B or Twilight #070105.
  • Glows are sized in percentages, so they scale with the element. On very wide or very tall areas, check the corners.
