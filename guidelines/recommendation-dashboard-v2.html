Recommendation · v2

Two corrections from v1. Crisp Orange and Eco Green are accents and never appear together, so every chart here is single-accent: one orange family plus neutrals, with a second series carried by value rather than a second hue. And the old green tint #b8d0d2 is gone — it read blue against the neutrals. Orange 70 #f8a48c takes its place as series 2.

Flint — light and dark

One neutral family, numbered steps, nothing else. Flint is the whole grey: 50 is the softer page, 100 the page, 200 does every recessed and ruled job, 300 is the border step that replaces Mid Grey, 400 is the mid-tone fill; 700/800/900 are the same material in dark mode. Oat, Ash, Flint Grey, Flint Deep, Chalk, Paper, Mineral and Stone are all retired — no warm variant, because Crisp Orange sits well on both white and Flint 100, and a second warm surface only muddied the page.

Mid Grey #d1d3d4 · Flint 300 · Flint 400 — the cool brand grey against the two warm-neutral UI steps
Where each one goes
Flint 50 #F9F9F6 — Flint 100 at half strength, halfway to white. Use it where a full page of 100 feels heavy: dense tables, long forms, or a page that is mostly white cards. One or the other per screen, never both. Flint 300 #C6C5BA replaces Mid Grey in the UI. Mid Grey #d1d3d4 is a cool grey — a faint blue cast that fights Crisp Orange and makes a border look like a different system. Flint 300 sits a step darker and warm-neutral, so it separates cleanly from Mid Grey and belongs to the same material as the page. Mid Grey stays a brand palette colour for the logo, print and brand-led surfaces; it is no longer a control border. Flint 100 / 200 / 300 / 400 — light mode: page, recessed fills and tracks, borders and dividers, mid-tone fill. Flint 200 carries progress tracks, quiet chips and the update block; 400 is a chart and disabled fill only — never a table header or a text ground. Flint 900 / 800 / 700 — dark mode: page, surface, rule. Same material, no second family. Content sits on white. The page tint only shows between cards, and neutrals stay secondary — a rule, a track, a disabled fill — supporting the orange rather than filling space. Table headers are white with a Flint 300 rule and muted labels, not a grey band.
