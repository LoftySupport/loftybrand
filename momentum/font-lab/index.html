Recommendations

My recommendations for the most readable Lofty UI. They are inference from the measurements below and design judgement, not decisions. The Apple points come from my own understanding of Apple's Human Interface Guidelines and were not re-checked against the current text today.

Sample text is invented for the test. Fieldwork is supplied in Light (300) and DemiBold (600) only, so weights 400 and 500 show Light. Faces other than Fieldwork load from Google Fonts (open licence); Fieldwork loads from this repository's assets/fonts and is for Lofty's own use. The grounds are the Hub's (app/src/lofty-ui/surfaces.css): Sunrise #fcf1ee with orange and Current glows, Deep Eco #020a0b and Twilight #070105 with the night glows, under 42% (light) or 5% to 8% (dark) white glass. “Glows 15%” caps every glow at 15% opacity, the Momentum decision of 6 October 2026. “Solid cards” keeps the 15% glows and puts text on opaque surfaces (Sunrise white, Deep Eco #0a1b1d, Twilight #170710), as the Momentum rule for body text already says. “Readable set” is a proposal, not a token: dark ink #eef4f3, secondary text 86%, body weight one step heavier on dark, light-theme muted #3e4d50.
