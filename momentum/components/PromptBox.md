# PromptBox

The hero of every landing screen: one large box to ask or instruct.

- 720px wide on desktop landing, a single-line docked version (64px) under a conversation, a pill bar on phones.
- `surface-input` fill, `line-control` edge, `radius-xl`, `shadow-float`, backdrop blur `blur-input`.
- Placeholder in `prompt` style: "Ask about any job, or tell Lofty what to do".
- Tool chips bottom left (Attach, job picker, Search records) as ghost pills; voice and send bottom right. Send is the one orange action.
